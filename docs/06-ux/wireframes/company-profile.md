## Notes

- Shariah badge (✅/❌) is always visible in the header regardless of active tab, linking
  to the Shariah tab.
- Tabs switch content in place (no full page reload) — Fundamentals/Technicals/Shariah
  wireframes describe each tab's content.
- Search bar persists across all Company Profile pages for quick re-search.
